# 30 minutes before the interview

### Minute 0–5: prepare the environment

Confirm editor/compiler, camera/mic, meeting link, scratchpad, and internet. Have water nearby. Do not begin an unfamiliar topic.

### Minute 5–12: coding warmup

Pick one pattern you know: sliding window, BFS, or hashmap. Explain brute force, optimization, a loop invariant, time/space, and two edge cases. Implement a small skeleton without looking.

### Minute 12–20: Java recall

Review [collections](../java/collections.md), [concurrency](../java/concurrency.md), and [the Java checklist](10-minute-java.md). Explain `hashCode`, `equals`, `LinkedHashMap`, thread safety, and `volatile` out loud.

### Minute 20–26: design framework

Review [the design framework](../system-design/framework.md), then narrate one design in 90 seconds: requirements → API → schema → architecture → one bottleneck → failure handling.

### Minute 26–30: behavioral opener

Prepare a concise project story in your own words: what problem, your role, the technical decision, why you made it, what you measured, and what you'd improve. Use only information you may share publicly.

**During coding:** confirm assumptions, give the simple approach first, then optimize, and reserve a few minutes to test. If instructed not to execute code, still talk through concrete cases.
